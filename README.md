# Inspecting Currents

Visual analytics interface developed for **Mini-Challenge 2 (MC2)** of the **VAST Challenge 2025**, as an exam project for the **Visual Analytics** course (A.Y. 2025/2026).

## Challenge context

Oceanus is a small archipelago whose economy has historically been based on **fishing**, but which in recent years has seen significant growth in the **tourism** sector. The local government established a commission, **COOTEFOO**, tasked with monitoring the island's economy and its initiatives.

Two organizations accuse the commission of being biased:

- **FILAH** (*Fishing is Living and Heritage*) claims that COOTEFOO favors tourism at the expense of fishing;
- **TROUT** (*Tourism Raises OceanUs Together*) claims the opposite, that the commission favors fishing at the expense of tourism.

A journalist, **Moray**, obtained both the data collected by FILAH and by TROUT, as well as a third, more complete dataset (**journalist**) including information absent from both. The goal of the project is to build a visual analytics tool that helps the journalist **verify the validity of these accusations**, comparing the three versions of the data and surfacing any bias — both in the *tone* of reported opinions and in their *coverage* (who/what is omitted).
